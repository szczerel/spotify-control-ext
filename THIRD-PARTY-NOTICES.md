## Lucide icons

The shuffle and loop icons in the `icons` folder (`repeat-off.svg`, `repeat-context.svg`,
`repeat-track.svg`, `shuffle-off.svg`, `shuffle-on.svg`) are **modified versions of icons from
[Lucide](https://lucide.dev)** (the `repeat`, `repeat-1`, `shuffle` icons and their "off" variants).
They were changed (colors, stroke widths, redrawn "off" states and file structure) for use in this extension.
